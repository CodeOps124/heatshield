# Demo video script (2–3 minutes, screen recording of the LIVE site)

Record at 1280×800 or larger. Keep captions on screen so it works muted. Use the live URL only
(no localhost). Everything below exists and was verified on 2026-09-28.

| Time | Screen | Say (or caption) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home page hero | "A weather app tells you it's 41 °C. It doesn't tell a delivery rider in Dubai or a grandmother in Delhi what to do about it, in their language. HeatShield does." |
| 0:15–0:45 | Click **Dubai · Arabic** example. Scroll through the result. | "Live forecast, turned into the US Weather Service heat index. Right now it's Danger in Dubai, even at night, because of the humidity. HeatShield shows this outdoor worker's risky hours: now until 4 a.m. tomorrow." Hover the 24-hour chart. |
| 0:45–1:15 | The **Your action plan** card, Arabic, right-to-left. Switch the language dropdown to **Hindi**, then **Spanish**. | "Amazon Bedrock writes a specific plan for this person: three steps with clock times and the warning signs to watch, grounded in CDC guidance. Switch language and it's regenerated, not translated from a table." Point at the provenance line (model name, cache). |
| 1:15–1:35 | Change profile to **Older adult (65+)** on a milder place (e.g. **Delhi · Hindi**). | "Same weather, different body, different threshold: older adults are warned one level earlier." |
| 1:35–2:05 | Open the **live demo dashboard**. | "The Community lane: one foreman, teacher or clinic worker watches a whole group. Ten people in ten real hot cities, live, worst first: who needs attention now and when it peaks." |
| 2:05–2:25 | Show the **alert email** in the inbox (from the manual trigger). | "And they don't have to remember to check. Every hour EventBridge re-scores everyone and Amazon SNS emails people whose risk reaches their level." |
| 2:25–2:45 | README architecture diagram, then the live URL on screen. | "Fully serverless on AWS: CloudFront, Lambda, DynamoDB, Bedrock, EventBridge, SNS — one SAM template, built with Claude Code connected to AWS through the AWS MCP Server. HeatShield: from forecast to action, in your language." |

Tips
- Open the Dubai link once before recording so the guidance is cached (instant on camera).
- If an action plan shows "Pre-written safety guidance", that is the offline fallback working —
  fine to show, but for the video pick a language that shows "Generated by … on Amazon Bedrock".
