    How the agents work together

    1. Every hour · Sol

      Finds heatwaves

      Compares the next days with each area's own 1991–2020 climate (Excess Heat Factor) and the heat index, then briefs the team. The algorithm caps how severe Sol may call an event.

    2. Every plan · Mira

      Writes the advice

      Retrieves only the relevant vetted CDC/NIOSH facts (BM25) and writes a plan in the person's language.

    3. Every plan · Lexi + Vera

      Check it before anyone sees it

      Lexi verifies the language (naive-Bayes language ID, back-translation); Vera checks safety (rule detectors, rubric). Blocking issues go back to Mira.

    4. Every 3 hours · Kai

      Plans check-ins

      Scores urgency and schedules who a group leader should contact first, earliest deadline first, across time zones.

    5. Every 15 minutes · Otto

      Keeps it up

      Probes the live site and the weather provider, runs control charts on latency, checks every agent's heartbeat, and writes incident reports.

    Models: Amazon Nova 2 Lite for Sol, Kai, Otto and (until Anthropic access is enabled on this account) Mira; Amazon Nova Pro for the reviewers, so a different model checks the writer's work (Nova Pro writes a plan only if Nova 2 Lite fails twice). Names like "Sol" are roles, not people; the agents give safety information, not medical care.

    Agent runs are recorded in Amazon DynamoDB and shown here without personal data: places are cities, and group plans never leave the leader's private dashboard.
