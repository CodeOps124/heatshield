A weather app tells you it’s 41 °C.
HeatShield tells you what to do about it.

Heat is linked to an estimated 489,000 deaths a year. The people most at risk — outdoor workers, older adults, anyone without reliable cooling — rarely get a warning that is specific to their body, their job and their language. HeatShield turns the forecast into a personal action plan and warns you before the hottest hours arrive.

How it works

Real weather, real heat science, and generative AI used for the part that matters most: turning a number into a plan a person will actually follow.

  1. 1 · Forecast

    Live hourly weather

    Temperature and humidity for the next four days from Open-Meteo, anywhere on Earth. Your location is rounded to about 1 km.

  2. 2 · Science

    NWS heat index

    The US National Weather Service formula combines heat and humidity into a heat index and risk tier, hour by hour, including when risk will rise and peak.

  3. 3 · Personal

    Your body, your threshold

    Older adults, pregnant people, young children and people with chronic conditions are warned one tier earlier than the general public.

  4. 4 · Amazon Bedrock

    An action plan in your language

    An AI model writes three concrete steps and the warning signs to watch, grounded in CDC and NIOSH guidance, in any of 13 languages.

  5. 5 · Early warning

    Alerts you don’t have to ask for

    Every hour, Amazon EventBridge Scheduler triggers a re-check and Amazon SNS emails anyone whose risk is rising.
